How It Works

Step 1: Connect

Enter the IP address or hostname of your remote computer. RemoteSupervisor establishes a secure connection using Apple's Network.framework.

Step 2: Capture

Activate screen sharing on the remote side. ScreenCaptureKit delivers a live, high-resolution view directly to your Mac.

Step 3: Control

Interact seamlessly. Use your keyboard and trackpad to navigate, execute commands, and manage the remote system in real-time.

Step 4: Disconnect Securely

End sessions with one click. All connections close cleanly, leaving no traces.
